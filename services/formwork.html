Formwork & Shoring

Our Expertise

Formwork & Shoring Hire Across Queensland

Prymehire stocks a comprehensive range of formwork and heavy-duty shoring equipment for hire to builders and contractors across QLD. From standard slab formwork to high-load shoring towers, we have the system and the inventory to meet your project needs without lead time.

Our fleet includes industry-standard Powershore and propping systems maintained to the highest compliance standards. Our team can advise on system selection, loading requirements and Safe Work Method Statements — making it easy to get the right gear on site, on time.

  • Large formwork and shoring inventory on hand
  • System selection advice from experienced staff
  • All equipment inspected and tagged to AS/NZS
  • Fast delivery across QLD

Capabilities

Formwork & Shoring Systems We Supply

Slab Formwork

Standard and engineered slab formwork systems for residential, commercial and civil projects.

Shoring Towers

Heavy-duty Powershore and equivalent shoring tower systems for high-load applications.

Column Formwork

Circular and rectangular column formwork systems available in a range of dimensions.

Acrow Props

Large stock of acrow props for temporary propping and falsework applications.

Wall Formwork

Panel and gang-form wall systems for core walls, retaining structures and tilt construction.

Accessories & Hardware

Full range of formwork hardware, clamps, soldiers and walers available with every hire.
