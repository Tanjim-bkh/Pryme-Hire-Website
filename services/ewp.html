EWP & Access

Our Expertise

Elevated Work Platform Hire in Queensland

Prymehire offers elevated work platform hire across Queensland, providing scissor lifts, boom lifts and vertical mast lifts for construction, maintenance and industrial applications. Our EWP fleet is well-maintained, regularly inspected and available for short and long-term hire.

Whether you need indoor access on a tight commercial fitout or heavy-duty boom reach on an industrial site, we can supply the right machine. Our team will advise on the right EWP for your application, site conditions and access constraints.

  • Scissor lifts, boom lifts and vertical mast lifts
  • Indoor and outdoor rated machines
  • Regular maintenance and inspection records
  • Delivery and pickup across QLD
