About Us

Who We Are

Queensland's Most Trusted Hire Partner

Prymehire is a Queensland-based construction equipment hire company servicing builders, subcontractors and project managers across QLD. We provide scaffolding, formwork, shoring and elevated work platform equipment to projects of every size — from residential builds to major civil and commercial construction.

Our fleet is large, maintained and compliant. Our team is experienced and hands-on. When you hire from Prymehire, you get equipment that shows up on time and a team that answers the phone.

  • Full hire fleet across scaffolding, formwork, shoring and EWP
  • Rapid mobilisation to sites throughout QLD
  • In-house equipment maintenance and compliance tagging
  • Dedicated account support from enquiry to collection

Safety & Compliance

Our Commitment to Safety

WHS Compliance

All hire operations are conducted in full compliance with the Work Health and Safety Act and applicable QLD regulations. Our safety management framework is reviewed and updated continuously.

Safe Work Method Statements

Comprehensive SWMS are available for all high-risk hire and installation activities. Each SWMS is reviewed with workers before commencement.

AS/NZS Maintained Equipment

Our entire fleet is regularly inspected, maintained and tagged to current Australian and New Zealand standards. Every item dispatched is compliant.
