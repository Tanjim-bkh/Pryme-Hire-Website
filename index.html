Who We Are

Queensland's Equipment Hire Specialists

Prymehire is a Queensland-based construction equipment hire company providing scaffolding, formwork, shoring and EWP hire to builders and contractors across QLD. We operate with a large maintained fleet, fast delivery, and a hands-on team that understands construction programs.
