Sales & Hire

Equipment Available

Scaffold & Formwork Equipment for Sale and Hire

We carry a comprehensive range of scaffolding and formwork equipment available for both sale and hire. Our yard stocks industry-leading systems in large quantities — maintained, tagged and available for rapid dispatch to your project site across Queensland.

Kwikstage

For Sale & Hire

Versatile modular scaffolding widely used in construction and industrial applications. Hook-on connections allow rapid, tool-free erection by a small crew — preferred for speed-critical projects. Available in standard bay widths and heights with a full range of accessories.

PERI UP Rosett

For Sale & Hire

Flexible and safe modular scaffolding engineered for complex structures — high load capacity and exceptional adaptability to irregular floor plans and facades. Fully compliant with AS/NZS standards.

Powershore 30

For Hire

Heavy-duty shoring system for high-load formwork support in large commercial, civil and industrial projects. Superior load ratings relative to its weight mean faster erection and reduced crane dependency.
